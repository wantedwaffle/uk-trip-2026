# UK, 28 November–6 December 2026

A reference route, not a script. Weather, a late coach, hunger, or a closed road can override it. The map is `index.html`.

## Conclusion

Two fuller London days, one Nottingham evening, then a driving loop that follows your Highlands PDF only where December daylight still exists. The car is picked up at Sixt Glasgow City after lunch on 1 December and returned at Edinburgh Airport before 18:00 on 4 December. Cardiff on the 5th is deliberately empty. The 6th is a castle, a steak, and a train to Heathrow Terminal 4.

Shellfish is off the list. Fish and chips is fine.

Three things on the tickets do not match the public timetables. Check them before you treat the days as settled.

1. **MH2** in the winter schedule leaves Kuala Lumpur at 23:30 and lands Heathrow Terminal 4 at **05:35 the next morning**. Saturday 28 November in London is this plan only if your ticket arrives that morning. If it departs KL on the 28th, you land on Sunday and the Saturday market moves.
2. The **4:30 Nottingham–Birmingham coach** is not on the National Express 150 timetable that started 30 October 2026. That timetable shows 08:55 and 15:00, which both miss an 08:45 flight. Older listings had a 4:30 from Broadmarsh arriving Digbeth at 6:45. If it is not on your ticket, taxi to Birmingham Airport. The drive is about an hour.
3. **20:20 Edinburgh–Bristol** is not on the published Friday 4 December easyJet list. That list has 17:45 (Bristol 19:00) and 21:00 (Bristol 22:15). A 22:15 landing does not make the last Bristol–Cardiff train reliable. Book a taxi from Bristol Airport to the friend’s place at Cardiff University.

## How the days go

### Saturday 28 November — flowers, Winter Wonderland, lights

Land Terminal 4, free train to Terminals 2 & 3, Elizabeth line to Liverpool Street, and walk to Columbia Road. You are on a backpack, so there is no hotel bag drop. Check in at ibis London City Shoreditch (5 Commercial Street) after dinner.

This is the tighter London day, three hops. Meet at Columbia Road Flower Market around 9:00. It only runs on Saturday. If it is pouring, meet inside Old Spitalfields Market. Lunch is an early Dishoom Shoreditch, booked, and you leave by 12:45.

Hyde Park Winter Wonderland is open 19 November 2026 to 3 January 2027. Saturday 28 November opens at 10:00. Book a 13:30 arrival hour. Two hours, not every ride. Bratwurst and mulled wine are fine. Skip seafood stalls. Weekday November tickets can be £1 off-peak. Saturday is not that price.

Leave the park by 16:15. Carnaby Street’s lights have been on since 4 November. Regent Street’s angels have been on since 5 November, lit 15:00 to midnight. Walk down to Piccadilly Circus and be on a train by 18:20. Oxford Street’s 2026 switch-on was not announced when this was written. Dinner is Manteca on Curtain Road at 19:30. Tower Bridge is the thing that comes out so this fits.

### Sunday 29 November — museum, then the river market

Leave the ibis with the backpack. British Museum for ninety minutes. It is free. Lunch at Bancone in Covent Garden. Covent Garden’s lights have been on since 12 November.

Somerset House courtyard is free to walk into. The rink runs 11 November to 10 January. Skate only if you have booked a session, about £13–£28.50 plus a £1.50 fee. Then walk the river to the Southbank Winter Market under Hungerford Bridge. It is free, stalls 11:00–22:00, from 2 November. The Google score on the market is only 3.9, so it is a walk and a snack, not a restaurant. Skip seafood stalls. Leicester Square has its own market and rink on the way back to Dean Street. Skating there is about £15 and optional.

Check in at Otherwander Soho (91–92 Dean Street) before dinner. Dinner is still Flat Iron on Beak Street. The Trafalgar Square tree is lit on 3 December, after you have left London. Christmas at Kew is the evening trail that does not fit, because it is in Kew and it deletes this dinner.

### Monday 30 November — train at 16:35

East Midlands Railway, St Pancras 16:35, Nottingham 18:18. Be on the concourse by 16:10.

The backpack comes to Westminster. Big Ben and Parliament, exterior only, then lunch at Dishoom King’s Cross. Coal Drops Yard’s Christmas lights have been on since 26 November. No left luggage, and no second Christmas market. Winter Wonderland opens at noon today, which is too late to do properly and still make a 16:10 concourse. The train is the anchor.

Nottingham Castle is shut by the time you arrive. One drink at Ye Olde Trip to Jerusalem, dinner at Bar Iberico in Hockley. You sleep at a friend’s place at University Park. Book a taxi tonight for about 4:10 from the campus to Broadmarsh. The coach does not pass the university. Kushi-ya is closed on Monday.

### Tuesday 1 December — Glasgow, then Fort William

4:30 coach if it exists, taxi Digbeth to Birmingham Airport, EZY434 at 08:45, Glasgow 09:50. Taxi to Sixt at Q-Park, 101 Waterloo Street. The airport desk is the wrong branch. Staffed hours are posted 8:00–16:00.

Lunch at Paesano, 94 Miller Street, the number 7. Leave the city by 13:15. Sunset at Fort William is 15:46. Luss, on Loch Lomond, gets the daylight. Glen Coe is a drive. The PDF’s 17:00 stop at the Three Sisters is after dark, so it is cut as a stop. Sleep at The Garrison on the High Street. The backpack stays in the car. Dinner at the Crofter, a short walk.

### Wednesday 2 December — the long Highland day

Glenfinnan for sunrise at 8:33. The Jacobite steam train does not run in December. Eilean Donan opens at 10:00, tickets on the day, last entry 15:00. It is open on the 2nd. It is closed on the 4th, which does not affect you.

Bags stay in the car at Eilean Donan. Rucksacks are not allowed inside the castle. That is a site rule, not a storage stop.

Lunch at Cafe Arriba in Portree. Old Man of Storr from the car park, not the full hike. Kilt Rock only if you are early. Leave north Skye by 15:10. Portree sunset is 15:45. The night is The Loch Ness Inn at Lewiston, just past Urquhart, not Inverness. Check in, then dinner at Fiddlers in Drumnadrochit. Book it. The inn’s own kitchen is the backup if you skip the mussels. Scotch & Rye is only if you have already driven the extra 20 minutes into Inverness.

If you leave Eilean Donan after 11:45, or the road is icy, skip Skye and drive to the inn. Urquhart is next door in the morning.

### Thursday 3 December — Loch Ness and a booked distillery

You wake at the inn, five minutes from Urquhart. Parking has to be booked online. The gate opens at 9:30. Watch the loch, go in, leave by 10:45. Lunch at the Lock Inn in Fort Augustus from noon. Glenfiddich only if you hold an afternoon tour. Winter tours are about every 90 minutes and the visitor centre closes at 16:30. Walk-ins rarely get on. Skip the four-hour Milestones tour.

If there is no afternoon slot, cut Speyside and drive south so Pitlochry is in daylight. Do not add Blair Castle until you have checked it is open in December. Sleep at Westlands of Pitlochry, 160 Atholl Road. Dinner at the Old Mill Inn, a short walk. Queen’s View is 15 minutes away at dawn.

### Friday 4 December — Edinburgh, then the car goes back

Queen’s View at sunrise, 8:23. The Hermitage if the path is not ice. Park once in Edinburgh, near the castle. Do not drive the Royal Mile. Lunch at Devil’s Advocate. Edinburgh Castle on a timed ticket, in by 14:00, out by 15:30. Winter last entry is 16:00 and it closes at 17:00.

Leave the city by 16:15. Sixt Edinburgh Airport, Car Rental Centre, Ingliston. Follow the rental-return signs at the hotel roundabout. Do not follow sat-nav into the passenger drop-off. Staffed hours are 6:30–23:00, so 18:00 is inside opening.

Then the flight, and a prebooked taxi from Bristol Airport to the friend’s place at Cardiff University, Park Place. Not the station, and not the Bay.

### Saturday 5 December — you are in Cardiff

Nothing is scheduled. You are at the same friend’s place, a short walk from the castle and from Cardiff Central.

### Sunday 6 December — the flight home

Walk from Park Place to Cardiff Castle for 9:30, about 15 minutes. Winter Sundays open at 9:00. Lunch at Pasture on High Street at noon. Book it. Walk to Cardiff Central, about 15–20 minutes with the backpack, and be there by 13:45 for a train around 14:00 to London Paddington, about two hours. Elizabeth line to Heathrow Terminals 2 & 3, then the free train to Terminal 4. The Elizabeth line does not stop at T4.

MH1 departs Terminal 4 at 21:25. Aim to be there at 17:15. 18:30 is the late side. A 15:00 train from Cardiff is the spare, and it is the last comfortable one.

Outdoor, and droppable: Columbia Road, Winter Wonderland, Regent Street, the Southbank market, Somerset House courtyard, Luss, Glen Coe, Glenfinnan, the Storr, Kilt Rock, Queen’s View, the Hermitage.

Indoor replacements already on the route: Spitalfields Market, the British Museum, Glenfiddich, Edinburgh Castle, Cardiff Castle, the pubs.

December daylight on this route is about 7 hours. Fort William sunset on 1 December is 15:46. Portree sunset on 2 December is 15:45. Edinburgh sunset on 4 December is 15:41. Anything the PDF scheduled after 16:00 in the Highlands is a drive, not a viewpoint.

## What was cut, and why

- **Quiraing.** Past Kilt Rock, single-track, and the light is gone.
- **The full Storr hike.** It deletes the drive back to Loch Ness.
- **Three Sisters at 17:00.** The sun is down.
- **A Glasgow museum day.** Kelvingrove costs Loch Lomond.
- **Nottingham Castle inside, and anywhere outside the centre.** You arrive at 18:18 and leave at 4:30.
- **Speyside, if Glenfiddich has no afternoon slot.** The backtrack through Inverness is only worth it for a tour you are already on.
- **Arthur’s Seat, and a second Edinburgh meal across town.** The car is due by 18:00.
- **Cardiff Bay on Sunday.** It is the wrong direction from the station. Saturday is free if you want it.
- **The Jacobite.** It does not run in December. Glenfinnan is the viaduct.
- **Seafood restaurants.** Crannog, and any place whose reviews lead with mussels, crab, or prawns.
- **Sky Garden, unless you already hold a ticket.** It is a booking lottery on a day that already has two markets.
- **Tower Bridge on Saturday.** Winter Wonderland and Regent Street use that afternoon.
- **Christmas at Kew.** The best lights trail, and it is an evening in Kew. It deletes Shoreditch dinner or Soho dinner.
- **Winter Wonderland on Monday.** The gates open at 12:00, and the Nottingham train needs you at St Pancras by 16:10.
- **The Trafalgar Square tree lighting.** It is on 3 December, after you have left London.
- **Saturday 5 December.** You asked for it to stay empty.

## Why it sits this way

Saturday is the only Columbia Road day, so the morning stays east and the afternoon crosses to Hyde Park because that is when Winter Wonderland is open early enough to do with friends. Sunday’s hotel is in Soho, so the museum, the river market, and the steak are one walk. Monday’s only immovable object is the 16:35. The Highlands keep your PDF order, with the dark stops turned into driving. Friday is built backwards from an 18:00 car return. Sunday is built backwards from a 21:25 departure at Terminal 4.
